A letter for you

My dearest Railly,

Happy birthday, my love, my cutie, my sweety. I still smile when I remember the first time I saw you on a video call. Something in me simply knew.

You are beautiful, kind and loving, and you make ordinary days feel like a gift. When we talk at night and I sit in our garden park watching couples pass by, I do not envy any of them. I only imagine the day you and I will walk there together, hand in hand.

I do not know how to measure how much I love you, so I will promise what matters: I will try to love you gently, protect your peace, care for you, respect you, help you, and support the dreams that make you shine. Above all, I will choose honesty and loyalty. Your happiness is one of the most valuable things in my life.

I am Indian and you are Filipina, and I think ours is the loveliest combination: two stories, two cultures, one hopeful little “us.” My dream is to grow together with patience, and, if our hearts choose it, to call you my wife one day. For now, I want to ask with all the tenderness I can: will you be my girlfriend?

Thank you for being you, my cutie, my sunshine, my sweetest thought. The happiness you bring me is beyond anything I can measure.

Forever yours,
Mayur, your Moha
